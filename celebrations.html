GOOD TO KNOW

Before we begin.

How much notice do you need?+

Please enquire at least 10 days before the celebration. Your date is confirmed only after we have discussed the project and checked availability.

Can the table follow our event theme?+

The event theme and setting are part of the discussion. Send colour references, photographs of the space and any ideas for the presentation so we can shape the brief.

What should I include in my enquiry?+

Your city, venue, event date, number of guests and type of occasion. It also helps to know whether you are looking for desserts, table styling, or both.

How are dietary needs handled?+

Share all dietary requirements and allergies when you enquire. We will discuss what can be accommodated before the dessert selection is agreed.
